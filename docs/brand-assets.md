# Lina Studio brand assets

## Typography

- Display and editorial headings: Lora
- Navigation, controls, and body copy: Urbanist

Both families are self-hosted at build time through `next/font` with Latin Extended support.

## Palette

- Ivory: `#f8f5e4`
- Pale blue-gray: `#c1d1d8`
- Light neutral: `#e0dfda`
- Powder blue: `#9abde2`
- Taupe: `#ae9b8c`
- Warm oatmeal separator: `#eee9dc`
- Charcoal: `#3f3f3f`
